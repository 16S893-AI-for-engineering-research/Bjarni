who's behind this

Hi, I'm Bjarni.

PhD candidate at LAE — the Laboratory for Aviation and the Environment. I started in 2023, and yes, the PhD is taking its sweet time. My research is on alternative fuels for aviation, with a focus on high-level system considerations and pre-screening: which fuels are even worth taking seriously before anyone commits to detailed aircraft integration studies.

This site (and the TASOPT+ project it describes) is the natural next step: having screened fuels at a high level, the plan is to model how fuel choice and aircraft design pull on each other — the total system impact of switching what an airplane burns.

Outside of the lab: running, biking, skiing, and generally being a bit of a petrol head. Equally happy geeking out over geopolitics, supply chains, commodity markets, and how ideas actually spread through the world — the same systems instinct, just pointed at different systems.

how the research narrows

From "all fuels" to "this fuel, this aircraft"

Pre-screening is a funnel: start broad across every plausible alternative fuel, apply system-level filters (energy density, production pathway maturity, safety, infrastructure), and narrow down to the handful worth a real aircraft-coupled analysis. TASOPT+ lives at the narrow end of that funnel — and this project is what lets us push more candidates through it faster.

back-pocket project ideas

Two other systems problems I think about

Not aviation, not this PhD — but the same instinct for "model the whole system, find the leverage points" applies. Filed here for whenever there's time (there is never time).

Global residential solar policy simulator

A model spanning US counties, Canada, Europe, and Australia that ties together regulation, incentives, tax breaks, and cost structures for residential solar — showing how each policy lever actually lands on different stakeholders. Think an En-ROADS / C-ROADS-style public simulator, but for "should I put solar on my roof, and why does the answer change so much by zip code or postcode."

Grid buildout optimizer

A fast model of electricity grid stability that estimates the impact of adding a given generation or storage source, coupled to a techno-economic + financial layer using current renewable generation/storage capabilities. Goal: rank candidate projects by carbon abatement and IRR, while respecting grid stability and long-run average cost — including the rare, painful low-generation periods that simple averages hide.
